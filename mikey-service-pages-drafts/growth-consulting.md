## Who it is for

Agency founders whose operations are not keeping up with growth.

This page is for a founder who has leads coming in, but whose current systems, tools, and team routines make it hard to handle more work without mistakes, delays, or extra manual work.

It is not a page for a buyer who only wants a single tool set up, with no review of how the rest of the business works. That buyer fits a different service page better.

## The problem

The existing growth-consulting page puts it plainly: growth is not only about adding more leads at the top. It is about fixing the leaks in the system that handles those leads.

Many agencies scale into chaos because their systems, or the lack of systems, cannot handle the volume. Manual steps pile up. Client onboarding takes too long. Tools overlap. No one has a clear view of what to fix first.

## What is delivered

A written review and a clear plan, based on your current operations. Draft deliverables for Alfaz to confirm:

- A review of your current operations, from your tech stack to your team's standard operating procedures (SOPs)
- A review of your CRM choice, your lead pipelines, and your toolset, including where tools can be simplified to save cost
- A list of manual bottlenecks, in priority order, with what to fix first
- A written roadmap for moving to a higher-ticket, automation-led agency model, as described on the existing growth-consulting page

1. Discovery and audit. You share how work moves through your business today, what tools you use, and where it breaks.

3. Review and priorities. You review the draft together and agree on what matters first, and what can wait.
4. Final plan and next steps. You receive the final written plan, with next steps stated in plain terms.
