## The problem

Many business websites are slow to load, hard to change, or hard for search engines to read. Content is added without a clear page structure. Forms, pages, and contact steps are not planned as one path for the visitor.

The result is a site that exists, but does not help a visitor understand the offer, trust the business, and take the next step.

## Process in 4 steps

1. Discovery and audit. You share what the site must do, who it is for, and what content you already have. Alfaz reviews any existing site and notes what to keep, change, or remove.
2. Draft strategy. Alfaz drafts the page list, the visitor path, and the build plan, and you agree on scope before building starts.
3. Solution build. The site is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind, with Supabase where data or an admin function is needed. You review the site as it takes shape.
4. Final and support. The final site is handed over with a note on what was built and how content can be changed.
