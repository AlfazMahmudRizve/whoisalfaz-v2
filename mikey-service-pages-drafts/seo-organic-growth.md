## Who it is for

For businesses that publish content but do not see it indexed or ranked in a clear way, and for businesses that want their site structure, content topics, and technical checks handled as one piece of work.

## The problem

The SEO service page states the problem plainly: a site can publish many blog posts, but if the posts lack topical clustering, semantic entity depth, and fast search indexation, the content can sit in Google's crawl queue. The page also lists schema markup, semantic entities, and automated indexing pipelines as part of the work.

## What is delivered

Work is scoped per client. Based on the site, delivery can include:

- Topical clustering and a topical authority map for the site
- Programmatic entity clusters, where agreed in the scope
- Semantic entity work and schema markup, where agreed in the scope
- An indexing setup that connects a CMS to Google Search Console and IndexNow APIs, as described on the site under the name Zero-Touch SEO Indexing Engine
- A code-level Core Web Vitals audit, as described on the site, with a free audit tool also listed on the site

No ranking, traffic, or impression result is promised here.
