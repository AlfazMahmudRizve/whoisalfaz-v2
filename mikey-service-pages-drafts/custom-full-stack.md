## Who it is for

This service is for a team or business that has outgrown off-the-shelf software. The current service page describes the case plainly: you reach a point where you are fighting the tool instead of using it, or paying for several subscriptions to do one job.

It fits a buyer who needs an app built around their own revenue workflow, rather than a generic tool they must work around.

## The problem

Off-the-shelf tools can block a workflow when they do not match how a team sells, follows up, or serves clients. The result, as described on the current page, is extra subscriptions, manual work, and a tool that does not fit the job.

## What is delivered

A custom web application, built from the ground up in Next.js with backend logic, and tailored to the buyer's revenue workflow.

The current service page names these kinds of builds as examples:
- a client portal that auto-updates from your CRM
- a bespoke project management dashboard
- an AI data pipeline built for the AI automation agency business model

The exact features for a buyer are agreed in the strategy step below. No feature list is promised here beyond what the buyer and Alfaz agree in writing.

The current page also states the app is owned 100% by you. That ownership point is kept here as stated on the source page, and should be confirmed in the final contract wording before publishing.

## Process in 4 steps

These four steps follow the Execution Framework named on the whoisalfaz.me homepage: Discovery & Audit, Draft Strategy, Solution Build, and Final & Support.

1. Discovery & Audit
   Review the current tool, workflow, and the job the new app must do. Confirm what is not working with the current setup.

2. Draft Strategy
   Agree the scope, the app structure, and the tools it must connect to, before building starts. This is also where timeline and price are confirmed — see the placeholders below.

3. Solution Build
   Build the app in Next.js with backend logic, based on the agreed scope.

4. Final & Support
   Check the build against the agreed scope and hand it over. Support terms after handover: TODO-Alfaz.
