## What applies to your cuts

- **Hook in the first 1-3 seconds.** Start with the most interesting, unusual or unexpected part — no introduction, no context first. The hook decides whether people stay or swipe.
- **On-screen text on every clip.** Most people watch Reels muted. If there is no text on screen, they will not understand what the video is about and will scroll away. Our clip tool already renders word captions — same standard for OSC Studio cuts.
- **CTA on organic clips only.** A call to action on every post (comment a word, save, share, follow, bio link). Do NOT add CTAs to campaign-prescribed captions — Foltyn, DJ Tunez and Fundingpips captions are exact as briefed, leave them alone. Organic/trending clips can carry one.
- **Judge a clip by retention, saves and shares — not likes.** Likes are not the main signal any more. A video with 200 likes can hit 100k+ views while one with 2k likes dies.
- **Big topics over narrow niches.** GTA 6 itself is the big topic — keep leaning on that rather than narrowing to tiny moments.
- **Post less / leave gaps clashes with campaign pace.** The guide says 2-3 posts a week with 1-2 day gaps so posts do not compete. We post several a day across campaigns, so do NOT slow campaign posting on this basis.
- **Repost / repurpose winners once we have one.** Nothing over 161 views yet, so nothing to repost right now. When a clip does break out, repost it or cut a new hook onto it.
