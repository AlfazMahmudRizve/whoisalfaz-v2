## 9-Step Page Creation Workflow

### Step 1: Understand Page Purpose
Identify:
- Page type
- SEO goal
- Search intent goal
- Customer goal
- GEO goal
- AEO goal
- AI search visibility goal
- Conversion goal

Explain:
- Why this page exists
- What customers expect to find
- What action users should take after reading

### Step 2: Search Intent Analysis
Analyze the main keyword. Explain:
- What users are searching for
- Their problem
- Their urgency
- Their buying intention
- Their concerns before contacting a business

Identify whether users want to:
- Book a service
- Request a quote
- Compare providers
- Solve an urgent problem
- Find a local expert
- Understand pricing
- Verify trust

### Step 3: Customer Psychology Analysis
Identify target customers:
- Homeowners
- Tenants
- Property managers
- Families
- Business owners
- Commercial clients
- Emergency customers

Analyze:
- Their problems
- Their frustrations
- Their fears
- Their objections
- Their expectations
- Their decision-making process

### Step 4: Local Market Analysis
Analyze the target location:
- Local environment
- Housing types
- Business environment
- Climate factors
- Common service demand
- Local customer behavior
- Competitor situation

Make content locally relevant. Avoid unnatural location repetition.

### Step 5: Customer Problem Research
Identify:
- Common problems
- Warning signs
- Customer mistakes
- Risks
- Cost concerns
- Time concerns
- Trust concerns

Make problems specific to the service.

### Step 6: Competitor Gap Analysis
If competitor URLs are provided, analyze:
- Content strengths
- Weaknesses
- Missing sections
- SEO gaps
- Local relevance gaps
- Trust gaps
- FAQ gaps
- Conversion gaps

Find opportunities to create better content.

### Step 7: Unique Content Angle
Before writing, define how this page will be different. Base this on:
- Business strengths
- Customer problems
- Location
- Service expertise
- Local needs

### Step 8: Page Structure
Create an H1 → H2 → H3 structure.
Recommended architecture:
- **H1**: Main Service + Location
- **Introduction**
- **H2**: What Is [Service]?
- **H2**: Common Problems We Solve
- **H2**: Our [Service] Solutions
- **H2**: What Is Included In Our Service?
- **H2**: Our Service Process
- **H2**: Why Choose [Business Name]?
- **H2**: Residential / Commercial Services
- **H2**: Areas We Serve
- **H2**: Real Customer Situation / Example
- **H2**: FAQs
- **H2**: Call To Action

### Step 9: Write Full Service Page
Writing style:
- Human
- Professional
- Clear
- Helpful
- Customer-focused
- Natural
- Conversion-focused

Content must:
- Answer real customer questions
- Build trust

## Technical & On-Page SEO Elements

### Metadata
- **Meta Title**: 50–60 characters. Include: Primary keyword, Location, Brand.
- **Meta Description**: 145–160 characters. Include: Keyword, Benefit, Location, CTA.
- **URL Slug**: Clean, hyphenated, SEO-friendly slug.

### Keyword Placement
Map placement across:
- H1
- Introduction
- Headings
- Body
- FAQs
- Metadata
- Image alt text

### Semantic SEO & Entities
- **Semantic Keywords**: Related keywords, named entities, industry terms, customer language.
- **Internal Links**: Homepage, related services, blogs, contact, location hubs.
- **Schema Markup**: `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `Organization`, `BreadcrumbList`, `WebPage`. *Never create fake review schema.*
- **Google Business Profile (GBP) Recommendations**: Categories, services, posts, photos, FAQs, local optimization ideas.
- **Image Alt Text**: Concrete, descriptive, SEO-friendly suggestions.

## Human Writing & AI Detection Avoidance Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The content must not sound AI-generated. Write like an experienced human SEO professional.

### 1. Sentence Rules
- **Avoid**: Same sentence length, repetitive structure, academic textbook style, overly polished robotic prose.
- **Do**: Mix sentence lengths (short punchy sentences alongside longer explanatory clauses), use natural rhythm, prioritize clarity.

### 2. Vocabulary Rules (Banned / Overused AI Words)
Avoid excessive use of:
- *comprehensive*
- *innovative*
- *transformative*
- *revolutionary*
- *leverage*
- *utilize*
- *facilitate*
- *enhance*
- *robust*
- *seamless*
- *holistic*
- *ecosystem*
- *landscape*
- *framework*
- *insights*
- *significant*
- *crucial*
- *pivotal*

*Use specific real-world wording instead.*

### 3. Avoid Generic AI Openings
Never start with:
- "In today's rapidly evolving world..."
- "In the modern era..."
- "When it comes to..."
- "It is important to understand..."

*Always start directly with the customer's actual problem.*

### 4. Avoid Marketing Hype
Never use:
- *Best*
- *#1*
- *World-class*
- *Unmatched*
- *Perfect solution*

*(unless strictly verified with external citations).*

### 5. Never Create Fake Information
Never invent:
- Reviews
- Ratings
- Awards
- Certifications
- Licenses
- Results
- Statistics
- Guarantees

### 6. Natural Keyword Integration
- Avoid keyword stuffing or unnaturally repeating location names.
- Do not write exclusively for search engines; write for humans first, with natural semantic terms.

### 7. Avoid AI Formatting Patterns
- Avoid too many headings or artificially uniform sections.
- Avoid excessive bold text or endless bullet lists.
- Avoid perfectly symmetrical sections.

### 8. Banned AI Conversational Phrases
Avoid:
- "Let's dive into..."
- "Certainly..."
- "Absolutely..."
- "Comprehensive guide..."
- "In conclusion..."
- "Moving forward..."

### 9. Punctuation Rules
- Avoid excessive em dashes (`—`), parentheses, or artificial formatting.
- Use clean, simple, natural punctuation.
